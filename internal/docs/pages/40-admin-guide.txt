Admin guide

Admin mode
Click "Admin mode" in the header and confirm with your authenticator app, a passkey (where PLATYPUS_PASSKEYS_ENABLED allows them) or a recovery code. Admin mode lasts a limited time (PLATYPUS_ADMIN_MODE_DURATION) or until you leave it. Five failed attempts in a row lock your account; another admin can unlock it.

Second factors
Set them up under /me/security. Once you have one, adding or removing factors needs admin mode, so a stolen login session alone can't add an attacker's authenticator. Keep your recovery codes in your password manager.

Users
Everyone in the allowed email domains (PLATYPUS_ALLOWED_EMAIL_DOMAINS) can log in; new users have no access until they get a policy. Add people ahead of their first login under Users, or by email on a team's page. Disable an account to shut someone out at once; unlock accounts that were locked after failed attempts. The last active admin can't be removed or disabled.

Teams
A team is a group of users sharing policies. Add members on the team's page: pick from the list of everyone with an account, or type to narrow it by name or username. People who haven't logged in yet aren't on the list; they join once they have logged in for the first time. Addresses are never typed in full: a small typo in a typed address could hand access to the wrong person.

Policies
Build policies from rules (see "Access and policies"). The editor shows how many items each rule matches, so you can spot a pattern that is too broad or matches nothing. Four policies were created at setup and are assigned to nobody: Read-only all, SSM editor all, Secrets editor all and Full access.

Block all access
The block button on the admin overview shuts out everyone except admins at once and stops all reveals and changes, for admins too. Users see a notice with the contact address from PLATYPUS_BLOCK_CONTACT_EMAIL. Admins keep the admin pages, and unblock platypus there.

Sync
The worker refreshes the list of parameters and secrets from AWS every PLATYPUS_SYNC_INTERVAL and re-checks platypus's own access to every item once per PLATYPUS_ACCESS_RECHECK_INTERVAL. "Sync now" asks for a sync within a minute, optionally with a full access check, for example after changing platypus's IAM role.

Audit trail
Every action is recorded: who, what, which item, allowed or denied, when, from where. Filter it, page through it, or download it as CSV. The audit trail is read-only. "Delete old events" removes events older than PLATYPUS_AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS; platypus never deletes events by itself. Every event also goes to the logs (log_type "audit"); send those to a log store that keeps them.

Notes
Notes stay when their item disappears from AWS. The admin overview links to these orphaned notes.
